# Floor Tracker

**[bjessief-apps.github.io/Floor-Tracker](https://bjessief-apps.github.io/Floor-Tracker/)**

Floor Tracker is a browser-based app for running a warehouse unloading shift from a phone. Set up your doors at the start of a shift, add your crew, and move workers between trailers as the day goes. The board shows which doors are being worked, which are sitting empty, and how far along each trailer is. At the end of the shift it produces a summary and a PDF.

## What it does

- Sets up a shift by door number, trailer ID, and piece count
- Places workers into doors and moves them between doors with two taps
- Tracks which doors are being worked and which are sitting empty
- Times each trailer, with the clock pausing automatically when nobody is inside
- Records completion checkpoints from 10% to 100% on a trailer
- Shows progress as a fill bar on each door
- Keeps a running log of every placement, move, checkpoint, and completion
- Counts open and finished doors on a live counter
- Opens a list of working or finished trailers and lets you act on them from there
- Marks a finished trailer unfinished to put it back on the board
- Keeps a note on any door
- Reuses a door number once its previous trailer is finished
- Reviews a shift summary of lumpers, finished trailers, and rollovers
- Shows the percentage remaining on rolled-over trailers
- Totals the completed container piece count
- Saves a shift summary as a PDF
- Stores finished shifts and reopens them later
- Switches between dark and light themes

## Who it's for

Floor Tracker is for warehouse leads and supervisors who assign lumpers to trailers during a shift.
